Leticia

Family Perspective — Birth

I remember the day Leticia was born with great affection. I was nervous and anxious to meet my daughter. When I finally heard her first cry and could hold her in my arms, I was very emotional.

It was an unforgettable moment for me. Since that day, my life changed, and Leticia became a very important part of it. It was one of the happiest days of my life.

School Trajectory

My school trajectory began at Demeneck Belotto Municipal School, where I had my first years of study and experienced many important moments.

Later, I went to a state school, where I continued my studies until the 9th grade. It was a phase of great learning, new friendships, and experiences that were part of my life.

Currently, I study at IFPR, where I started a new stage of my school life. I am meeting new people, learning different things, and preparing for my future.

Historical Fact

On the day I was born, I had a lot of difficulty breathing and almost died. It was a very delicate moment, but fortunately, they managed to take care of me and I got better. Today, this story is part of my life and shows how my birth was a very remarkable moment.

Current Moment

At first, I had a little difficulty adapting to the new school and routine. Over time, I got used to it, meeting new people, and adapting better. Today, I am calmer and managing to enjoy this new phase of my school trajectory.

Future Perspectives

For my future, I plan to continue studying and go to college. I am still in doubt between Psychology and Veterinary Medicine, as they are areas that interest me a lot. I hope to choose one of them and follow a profession that I like.

City

I live in Toledo, Paraná. The city is known as the "Agribusiness Capital" of the state, with emphasis on pig farming and food production. One of the city's symbols is the National Roasted Pork Festival, which has been happening for over 30 years and is internationally recognized.

In addition to gastronomy, Toledo has a diverse culture, marked by the influence of Italian and German immigrants who colonized the region. The city has parks, green areas, and cultural spaces, such as the Municipal Theater and the Municipal Lake.

Neighborhood

I live in the Coopagro neighborhood, in Toledo. The neighborhood emerged in the 1980s and went through a long formation process until becoming one of the most populous regions of the city.

Today, Coopagro is known for bringing together several companies, educational institutions, and shops, which moves the local economy. Even with this growth, the neighborhood maintains a residential profile, with quiet streets and a community that values tranquility.

Coopagro is part of my routine and my history, being the place where I grew up and live different stages of my life.
