City

I live in Toledo, Paraná, a city known for its strong presence in agriculture and food production. The city is an important agro-industrial hub in the western region of the state and houses several companies in the food sector.

The park is one of the city's main tourist attractions, with trails, walking tracks, a municipal aquarium, and a zoo-botanical garden. In addition, Toledo has a strong economy focused on agribusiness and a diverse culture, resulting from the influence of different peoples who colonized the region.

Neighborhood

I live in a region close to São João Street, in Toledo - PR. This street is one of the important roads in the city and extends from the Center to the Jardim Gisela neighborhood.

The part of the street that is in the Center is a very busy region, with shops, services, and easy access to other areas of the city. The section that passes through Jardim Gisela has a more residential profile, with houses and buildings, being a quiet area to live in.

I like living here because it is a place where I feel good, with easy access to the center and other important points of the city. It is part of my routine and my history.
