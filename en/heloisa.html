Heloisa

Family Perspective — Birth

The birth of Heloisa was a very special and long-awaited moment for the family. It was a day full of emotions and expectations, which marked the beginning of a new phase for everyone around her.

Since then, I have followed her growth and could see each stage of her life up close. It is very rewarding to remember all these moments and realize how much she has grown and changed over the years.

School Trajectory

My school trajectory began in Elementary School I, at Henrique Brod Municipal School. It was where I lived my first years of school and began to build my experience with studies.

In Elementary School II, I started studying at Antônio José Reis Civic-Military College. It was a new stage in my school life, with new learning, experiences, and people.

Currently, I study at IFPR in Toledo, where I am living a new phase of my school trajectory and meeting different people and knowledge.

Historical Fact

When I was born, I needed some special care during my first moments of life due to an issue with my body's development. Among these cares, I needed to use a brace for a period. Fortunately, everything went well, and this situation became a remarkable part of my story.

Current Moment

Currently, I consider that I am living a good phase of my life. I have important friendships and people with whom I can share moments and experiences.

I am also adapting well to my routine at IFPR and taking advantage of the opportunities to learn new things.

Future Perspectives

For the future, I would like to continue my studies and go to college. I am mainly interested in the Exact Sciences area and plan to pursue some area of Engineering.

In addition to studies and career, I also have an interest in Cinema and Philosophy, which I would like to explore as hobbies and ways to discover new areas and develop my creativity.

City

I was born in Toledo, Paraná, where I currently live. The city is located in the west of the state and had its origin linked to the colonization of the region in the 1940s, when families of Italian and German descent arrived in the place.

Thanks to the fertility of the soil known as "purple earth", Toledo developed rapidly and today is recognized as one of the main agro-industrial hubs in Paraná. In addition, the city has parks, green areas, and leisure spaces, such as the Municipal Lake.

Toledo is a city that is part of my history and where I have been living different stages of my life.

Neighborhood

I live in the Jardim Panorama neighborhood in Toledo. Its history began in the 1980s, when the first families settled in the region. At that time, the neighborhood did not yet have complete infrastructure but grew little by little, attracting new residents.

One of the landmarks of Jardim Panorama is the Holy Family Parish, founded in 1983, which became an important meeting point and organization of the community. Over time, the neighborhood received important improvements, such as the repaving of several streets.

It is a quiet and residential neighborhood where I enjoy living and which is part of my daily life.
