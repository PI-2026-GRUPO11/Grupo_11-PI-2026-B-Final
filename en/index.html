TCTs - Contemporary Cross-Cutting Themes

Drawn Theme: Multiculturalism

Video about Cultural Diversity

Educational video about cultural diversity.

The theme drawn for Group 11 was Multiculturalism, focusing on the subtheme Cultural Diversity. This theme addresses the importance of respecting differences, valuing diverse cultures, and harmonious coexistence among different peoples who form society.

Below we present some of the actions, projects, and activities carried out in the city of Toledo - PR that promote diversity culture and respect for local traditions.

Actions in our city

1. FLIT (Toledo Literary Festival)

FLIT is one of the largest cultural events in Toledo, held annually by the Municipal Department of Culture. In 2026, the 5th edition took place between August 1st and 7th, at Praça Willy Barth and the Municipal Public Library, with a completely free schedule.

The event brings together a book fair, storytelling, workshops, lectures, round tables, cultural presentations, and meetings with authors. FLIT also promotes literary contests Paulo Leminski (short stories) and Edy Braun (chronicles and poetry), which in 2026 received more than 1,200 entries from authors across Brazil and other countries.

In addition, the event opens space for the community to participate actively through Open Microphone and FLIT Soiree, where anyone can present poetry, music, theater, and other artistic manifestations.

🔗 Check out the full schedule of the 5th FLIT on the Toledo City Hall website

2. Bon Odori (Japanese Culture Festival)

Bon Odori is a celebration of Japanese culture that is already in its 5th edition in Toledo. In 2026, the event took place on October 11, at the headquarters of the Toledo Cultural and Sports Association (CEATO), with free admission and 12 hours of programming.

The festival celebrates the Japanese tradition of honoring ancestors (Obon) through circular dances, presentations of taiko (Japanese drums), karaoke, cosplay, cultural workshops, and a wide variety of typical dishes in the Gastronomic Village.

The event included the participation of taiko groups from other cities, such as Maringá, Londrina, and Cascavel, as well as the Kotobuki Bon Odori dance group. In 2025, Bon Odori was officially included in the Official Calendar of Events of the Municipality of Toledo through Law No. 3,022.

🔗 Learn more about the 5th edition of Bon Odori

3. Virada Cultural

Virada Cultural is an annual event that celebrates art and culture in Toledo. In 2025, the 14th edition took place between December 5th and 7th, with more than 40 attractions distributed across different stages at Diva Paim Barth Ecological Park, Horto Municipal, and the Municipal Theater.

The program included musical performances of various styles (rock, gospel, reggae, samba), dances, children's theater, graffiti interventions, rhyme battles, and Viradinha Cultural, aimed especially at children, with storytelling, games, and a cinema session.

One of the highlights of the edition was the appreciation of local artists, who went through a public call and technical screening, in addition to national attractions such as Pato Fu and Sambô. The event also featured the Festival Emprega, which integrated culture and employability in a single space.

🔗 Read about the call for artists for Virada Cultural

4. Migrant Week

Migrant Week is an action to welcome and value cultural diversity promoted in Toledo, especially through the work of Embaixada Solidária, an organization that works to welcome migrants, refugees, and stateless people.

In 2026, Migrant Week was officially included in the Official Calendar of Events of the Municipality through Law No. 3,080/2026, sanctioned on March 30. The law provides for the annual holding of the event in the week comprising June 15, with activities such as lectures, documentary screenings, culinary workshops, multicultural lunches, photographic exhibitions, conversation circles, and friendly football matches.

Toledo hosts migrants and refugees from 46 countries, including Haiti, Senegal, Venezuela, Cuba, Syria, Afghanistan, and Ukraine, which makes Migrant Week an important space for integration and recognition of the contribution of these communities to local culture and economy.
